Section 105 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 ke according, ghair manqoola jaidad ki lease tareef, immovable property ka ek transfer hai jo kisi baad me ek time ke liye, express ya implied, ya phir perpetuity tak, hota hai. Is lease ka sabse pahla condition ye hai ki iska exchange koi price ya tole kiya gaya ho, ya phir money, share of crops, service ya kisi aur value wala thing ho. Transferor se transferee ko ye service ya thing periodic ya specific occasion par milta hai. Is tareef me transferee uss right ko accept karta hai jo uska transfer karta hai.
